What is beauty salon insurance?

Beauty salon insurance protects your business against the specific risks of providing beauty treatments. The core cover is public liability, but beauty businesses face an additional risk: treatment liability.

Treatment risk is non-negotiable. Standard public liability does NOT cover claims from beauty treatments going wrong (allergic reactions, chemical burns, infections). You need a provider that specifically includes treatment risk cover. This is the single most important detail to check.

If you employ staff, employers liability insurance is a legal requirement with a minimum of 5 million pounds. The NHBF recommends all salon owners carry public liability and treatment liability as a minimum.

  • Public liability (injury or property damage to clients and public)
  • Treatment risk (allergic reactions, burns, infections from treatments)
  • Products liability (claims from products you use or sell)
  • Employers liability (legal requirement if you have any employees)
  • Contents and stock cover (equipment, furniture, products)

What to look for in beauty salon insurance

Treatment cover
Check every treatment is listedSome providers exclude microblading, chemical peels, or laser work unless you hold specific qualifications and pay extra.
Mobile and home
Check you are covered outside a salonSome policies only cover a named premises. Accidental damage to clients' property is essential for mobile therapists.
Qualifications
Insurers require proof of trainingMany policies require recognised qualifications for specific treatments. Check your policy covers everything you are qualified to offer.

Product liability covers claims from products you use or sell. If a client has an allergic reaction to a facial cream or hair dye you applied, this is a product liability claim, not a treatment risk claim. Most specialist salon insurers include both as standard, but check the limits on each.

Common questions

Do I need insurance to run a beauty salon?
Public liability is not legally required but is considered essential. If you employ anyone, employers liability is a legal requirement. Most landlords and trade bodies require proof of insurance.
What is treatment risk cover?
Treatment risk covers claims from treatments going wrong: allergic reactions, chemical burns, infections, damage to skin, hair, or nails. Standard public liability does not cover these. Essential for all beauty businesses.
How much does beauty salon insurance cost?
Between 3 and 25 pounds per month for a sole trader, depending on treatments, cover level, and whether you have premises or work mobile.
Does my insurance cover working from home?
Not automatically. Check your policy explicitly covers home-based and mobile work. Protectivity and Salon Gold both offer policies designed for mobile and home-based therapists.
