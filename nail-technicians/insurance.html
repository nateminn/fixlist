What is nail technician insurance?

Nail technician insurance protects you against the specific risks of providing nail treatments. The core covers are public liability, treatment risk (claims from treatments going wrong), and products liability (claims from products you use or sell). If a client develops an infection, has an allergic reaction to gel polish, or suffers damage to natural nails, these are treatment risk claims that standard public liability will not cover.

The NHBF recommends all nail technicians carry public liability and treatment risk as a minimum. If you employ anyone, employers liability is a legal requirement.

What to look for

Treatment cover for your services. Check your policy covers gel nails, acrylics, BIAB, nail art, and any add-on services like waxing or lash extensions. Some policies exclude certain treatments.

Mobile suitability. If you are not salon-based, ensure your policy covers work in clients' homes, rented chairs, and events. Accidental damage to clients' property should be included.

Equipment cover. UV/LED lamps, e-files, and stock represent real investment. Check limits and whether it is included or an add-on.

Student cover. If you teach nail courses, look for a teacher training extension. Salon Gold and Professional Beauty Direct both offer this.

Common questions

Do I need insurance as a nail technician?
Public liability is not legally required but most salons and chair rental agreements require proof of insurance. Treatment risk cover is strongly recommended. If you employ anyone, employers liability is a legal requirement.
What does treatment risk cover?
It protects you if a nail treatment causes injury or illness: allergic reactions, infections, damage to natural nails, and burns from UV or LED lamps. Standard public liability does not cover these.
How much does nail tech insurance cost?
Between 3 and 15 pounds per month for a sole trader. The cheapest we found starts from 3.30 pounds per month with Professional Beauty Direct.
Am I covered working in clients homes?
Only if your policy covers mobile work. Protectivity, Salon Gold, and Professional Beauty Direct all offer mobile nail technician policies including accidental damage to clients property.
